We learned this running validators on Polkadot and Kusama: nodes that sign with keys worth protecting, are measured in public block by block, and are penalized for every outage and every double-sign. The validators are retired; the habits are not. Keys live in hardware, nothing depends on a single machine or provider, alerts fire before users notice, and every deployment can be rebuilt from its description.

gli.al is the infrastructure practice of [F.R.E.D.](https://fred.build), a studio in Zug, Switzerland, with a background in leading engineering and infrastructure for Substrate-, Ethereum- and Solana-based networks through to mainnet launch. The same care goes into a mail server, a build pipeline or a company network.
